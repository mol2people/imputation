## Bottom line

The September 18–19 work contains four distinct layers: the prespecified pooled experiment (v1), a later pooled v2 variant, a completed source-specific sidequest, and an additional tuned-500 extension. The main finding is modest discrimination of recorded salutation, with substantial class asymmetry. Source-specific models perform better in some cohorts, but they target narrower populations and cannot support vendor ranking.

### 1. Pooled experiment

The [original plan](./EXPERIMENT_PLAN.md:1) specifies salutation 10 vs 20, participant-level 80/10/10 splits, exclusions 38/46/48, one default random forest, and 10 fixed-prediction bootstrap resamples.

| Run | Eligible / split | Main difference | Validation AUROC | Test AUROC | Test balanced accuracy |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| v1 `artifacts/` | 20,485; 16,388/2,049/2,048 | Matches the written plan | 0.668 | **0.713** | 0.590 |
| v2 `artifacts_v2/` | 20,423; 16,339/2,042/2,042 | Also excludes 2/4/19; adds age/BMI groups and source indicators | 0.706 | **0.683** | 0.581 |

V1 is the canonical implementation of the plan. The full [v1 report](./REPORT.md:102) records:

- Coverage gate: at least 14 adequate days in one HR channel, with an adequate day requiring ≥8 observed clock hours and either ≥8 hours interval coverage or ≥60 measurements.
- Default 100-tree RF, trained once on training participants.
- Test class-10 recall only 0.255 versus class-20 recall 0.924. Thus 0.703 accuracy is partly driven by the 66.9% class-20 prevalence.
- Ten bootstrap draws gave mean AUROC 0.713, SD 0.016; this is a rough conditional variability check, not a confidence interval.
- All 15 saved integrity checks passed.

The [v2 model report](./artifacts_v2/model_report.md:1) shows similarly asymmetric classification: class-10 recall 0.246 and class-20 recall 0.915. V1 and v2 are not clean model comparisons because cohort exclusions, predictors, and allocations all changed.

### 2. Source-specific sidequest

Six feature variants were fitted separately per source: demographics only, recording-span, selected 91-day window, two rolling variants, and all 398 features. Baseline → tuned-500 test AUROC:

### Substantive result

- A1 hygiene produced no detected AUROC change: mean deltas ranged from −0.0016 to +0.0070 and every unadjusted 95% interval included zero. It reduced `all` from 534→400 features (source 3), mean 736→401 (source 6), and 623→401 (source 7).

- Under the narrower primary comparisons in my alternative plan, A2 on `all` was unsupported:

  - source 3: +0.00231, simultaneous-style 97.5% CI [−0.00127, +0.00588]
  - source 6: +0.00215, [−0.00113, +0.00542]

- A2 effects are small and source/variant-dependent; A3 is unstable and offers no consistent advantage. Thus “do not adopt A2 or A3 as defaults” is justified.
- This fits the earlier reports: regularization mattered substantially relative to sklearn defaults, while selection adds little once G1 is fixed. The remaining signal still looks predominantly like recording/wear-pattern information rather than physiology.

### Corrections needed in the interpretation

1. **Multiplicity does not disappear through pre-specification.** The report displays 90 contrasts. If treated as one family, Holm correction leaves only three A2-vs-A0 results: source-3 `rec` +0.0050, source-3 `win` +0.0035, and source-6 `roll_win` −0.0083. The other “significant” findings should be described as unadjusted exploratory intervals. See the current claim in [sidequest_report.md](./artifacts_sq/sidequest_report.md:214).

2. **One unadjusted result was omitted:** source-3 `roll_win`, A2−A0 = −0.0037 [−0.0068, −0.0006], shown in [feat_sel_report.md](./artifacts_sq/fsplit/feat_sel_report.md:28).

3. **The frozen-versus-repeated comparison cannot isolate “draw luck.”** It compares 90%-training/10%-test, 500-tree fits against 70%-training/15%-test, 100-tree fits. The ±2.7/2.3 SD values are descriptive standardized discrepancies, not estimates of frozen-fold luck. The claim that both protocol differences make both gaps conservative is especially invalid for the positive source-6 gap. Repeated splitting reduces dependence on one partition; it does not create new independent validation.

4. **The implementation differs from the active plan.** The plan specifies sorted feature-name order for correlation pruning and canonical tie-breaking, but the code scans transformed positional order and uses stable positional ties ([plan](./artifacts_sq/plans/sidequest_feat_sel_repeated_splits_2026-09-19.md:45), [implementation](./src/sidequest/feat_sel.py:130)). I would also add source-only and gate-only ablations: otherwise high `R` performance could be almost entirely device choice or the known class-dependent gate, rather than structured missingness. Numeric zero-variance filtering must retain columns that are constant when observed but sometimes NaN, and source-slice AUROC should require adequate counts in both classes, not merely total \(n\ge50\).

Finally, “informative observation process” is more accurate than “MNAR analysis.” The plan correctly acknowledges that it does not identify an MNAR mechanism or recover outcomes for the 11 unlabeled participants.
