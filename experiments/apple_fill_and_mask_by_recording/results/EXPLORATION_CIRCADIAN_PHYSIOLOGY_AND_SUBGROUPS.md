# Circadian physiology and subgroups: unverified exploratory report

Corrected on 2026-10-19. Use [REPORT.md](REPORT.md) for verified benchmark and recording-subgroup evidence.

[06_explore_circadian_physiology_and_subgroups.py](../scripts/06_explore_circadian_physiology_and_subgroups.py) computes observed-HR descriptors for allocation 0 training participants from the older Apple cohort caches (lines 129–150), but writes this report using literal numerical prose and tables (lines 258–334). The previous numerical claims were therefore not established as the output of those calculations. They have been withdrawn rather than presented as verified empirical findings. No cache summaries or models were rerun during reconciliation.

Associations between occupancy and observed HR cannot by themselves distinguish selective sampling from differences in participant physiology, activity, or device behavior. They do not prove MNAR or establish a causal off-wrist mechanism. Such associations also cannot establish why a filling arm changes discrimination.

The observed-HR 10th percentile is an operational summary, not a validated resting-HR measure. Clock windows do not establish sleep. Mean absolute changes between adjacent observed 5-minute bins are not measurements of beat-to-beat HRV or respiratory sinus arrhythmia. The previous 10%/20% HR-dipping categories were not validated as clinical phenotypes in this analysis. These limitations apply to any later reuse of the underlying descriptive calculations.
